# Quote file — vetted (public version)

> **Public version.** Rows from Mike's original quote file that were marked **DROP** are listed by ID only; their text is omitted. Many were unattributed social-media lines or partisan remarks that this project does not endorse. Every quotation the paper uses is a KEEP or CORRECT row below.

**Verdicts:** **KEEP** (accurate; usable as is, with the source added) · **CORRECT** (usable after the fix shown) ·
**DROP** (false, unsourced, or would cost credibility with a careful reader) · **PENDING** (unattributed text that may
be Mike's own; Mike should say whose it is) · **N/A** (not a quote, e.g. a link or image caption).
**Confidence** refers to the attribution and facts, not to whether the quote is persuasive.

> **Corrections to v1.** v1 was built from a truncated copy and reconstructed 13 quotes. Against the real file:
>
> - **The Krauss quote is not the "Forget Jesus" line.** It's his gentler "A universe without purpose should neither
>   depress us…" (Q6). The v1 verdict was wrong.
> - **The Gervais quote is not the Colbert "holy books" line.** It's his 2015 "nearly 3000 Gods" tweet (Q22).
> - **The Carlin material is three quips (verbatim from an FFRF profile) plus the "bullshit… Religion" line** (Q70–71),
>   not the "invisible man" routine.
> - **The Lennon quote is longer than reconstructed and clearly fake.** It includes "Love and quantum physics are my
>   religion" (Q69).
> - **"Consciousness creates reality" and "every subatomic particle displays consciousness" are in two different
>   items** (Q3, unattributed; Q20, Ram McConaghay).
> - **v1's A3 ("The problem with religion…") is now resolved as Q3.**
>
> v1's verdicts on the Wolsey, Lao Tzu, Clifton, galaxies/sand, Grok, Sacks, Evans, and partisan items stand.
